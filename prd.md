# Website Brief

This document captures what you want your website to say, do, and feel like. Write as much or as little as you need — the more context you provide, the better the result. Anything you leave out, we'll figure out together during the build.

The only things you **must** provide are the name of the site and enough about your goals and messaging that we can build something meaningful. Everything else — structure, layout, exact copy, colours — can be developed as part of the process.

## Messaging & Content

*What should the site actually say?*

This is the heart of the brief. Describe what you want to communicate, in whatever way makes sense to you. You can write polished copy, rough notes, bullet points, or just describe what each part of the site should convey and we'll develop the copy together.

Some things to think about:

- **First impression** — When someone lands on the site, what's the first thing they should see? What's the headline message? What should they do next?
- **Key messages** — What are the 2–6 most important things you want to communicate? These might be benefits, differentiators, capabilities, or values.
- **The ask** — What do you want visitors to do? Fill out a form? Send an email? Book a call? Download something? How prominently should this be presented?
- **Your story** — Is there an "about" narrative? Who are you, how did you start, what do you believe in?
- **Proof points** — Do you have testimonials, client logos, case studies, statistics, or awards to feature?
- **Products or services** — Do you need to describe specific offerings? How detailed should this be?

## Site Structure

*What pages or sections does the site need?*

List the pages you want, with a short note about what each one should contain. Don't worry about getting this perfect — pages can be added, removed, or reorganised later through the CMS. If you're not sure about structure, just describe the content you have and we'll suggest a structure.

Some common patterns to consider:
- A homepage with your core message and key highlights
- An "about" page telling your story
- A "services" or "what we do" page
- A contact page or section
- A blog or news section
- Team profiles
- Case studies or portfolio
- FAQ
- Pricing
